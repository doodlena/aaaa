4. Registration and appointment process
LHR uses an external system called SmartChoice. Their website describes the following workflow.

Loudoun Hunger Relief
+2
1. Apply for assistance. New members complete the online application. Existing members do not need to apply again.
2. Receive a Shopper ID and PIN. LHR asks applicants to allow two business days after submitting their application.
3. Schedule an appointment. Members use their ID and PIN on the appointment page to select a location and time.
4. Attend the appointment. Members receive email and text confirmations and follow the instructions for their selected appointment type.
5. Return for future visits. Regular members can schedule two visits per month, but never more than once per week.
