Open doors. Full shelves. Here for our neighbors.

No family should have to choose between food and rent. Our Hunger Resource Center supports more than 600 families each month with emergency food assistance, nutrition information, and resources for healthy eating across Prince William County, Manassas, and Manassas Park.

Families shop through a client-choice shopping model, selecting the foods that work for their household. No income verification required.

Get food assistance

Registration required. Here's how to get started.
Step 1
Register as a new client

Call

571.748.2680

to schedule a NEW CLIENT REGISTRATION appointment and leave a message. We receive a high volume of calls so please be patient and we will return your call to confirm an appointment time.

New client registration appointments are held 

every Wednesday & Friday

1:00 - 2:30 PM

By appointment only

For your appointment, you’ll need to bring:

  • Valid photo ID
  • Proof of Prince William County residency issued within the past 30 days

No photocopies or expired documents accepted. If you are currently homeless or in temporary housing, please call us to discuss your situation before coming in.

Step 2
Schedule food pickup appointments

Once registered, you can schedule food pickup appointments during the following hours:

    • Tuesdays: 9:30 – 11:30 a.m. and 1 – 3 p.m.
    • Every other Wednesday: 5 – 7:30 p.m.
    • Thursdays: 9:30 – 11:30 a.m. and 1 – 3 p.m.
    • Every other Friday: 9:30 – 11:30 a.m.

    No photocopies or expired documents accepted. If you are currently homeless or in temporary housing, please call us to discuss your situation before coming in.
