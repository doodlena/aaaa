# Lantern for food pantries and service providers

Lantern lists free food, health and support services in Northern Virginia. If you run or volunteer at one of them,
you can keep your listing accurate and tell families what is happening today. It's free.

**Contact:** alexandercarsoncordova+lantern@gmail.com (questions, access problems, or to restore removed access). The
site shows the current address on the "For services" page and in the footer; deployments set it with
`FOODACCESS_CONTACT_EMAIL`.

## Getting access

1. **Create a free account** ("For services" → *Create a free account*). Use your work email if you have one;
   signing in with Google also works.
2. **Find your listing.** Search by address or ZIP, type part of the name, and choose *Request access*. Tell us your
   role and how we can confirm you, for example "Call the church office at 703-…, ask for Pastor Kim".
3. **We confirm with your organization.** Someone from the site team calls or emails your organization using its
   public contact details. This usually takes a few days. Your request shows *Waiting for review* until then; you can
   withdraw it.
4. **Approved or not.** Once approved, the listing appears under *Your services*. If a request is declined, the
   reason appears on your portal page and you can send it again with better details.

Your listing isn't on the site? Email the contact above with its name and address.

## Posting updates

Open your listing from *Your services*.

| What | Where it shows | Reviewed first? |
|---|---|---|
| Today's status (open, limited, out of food, closed) and what's on the shelf | Public listing and search, labelled "Posted by the pantry". Ends at midnight (or up to 3 days) | No |
| Special distributions and events | Public listing, calendar export | No |
| Closures (holiday, weather) | Removes that day's hours everywhere | No |
| Corrections: hours, requirements (ID, proof of address…), phone, email, website, languages, wheelchair access | Public listing **after** the site team accepts them | Yes |
| Replies to reports from families | Admin and your dashboard | No |
| Surplus food for other pantries | Surplus board (approved staff only) | No |

**Paste your announcement** (if AI is turned on): paste your flyer text or social post and Lantern drafts the matching
form for you. Nothing is posted until you check it and press post.

**Confirm your listing is correct.** If everything on your public page is right, press *Yes, it's all correct*. The
listing then shows as verified with today's date. Wait until any corrections you sent have been reviewed.

## Your team

Under *People who can post*, add colleagues by the email of their Lantern account (they create a free account first;
we don't send emails). You can remove anyone except yourself. The site team can also remove access.

## Privacy

We keep your email, your role and what you post. We never sell or share it. Families' reports don't include their
names.
